About

Hi, I'm Parshya.

I'm a communicator based in Richmond, Virginia, where I live with my two cats — my most loyal (and most demanding) audience.

Music has always been a big part of my life. In college at the University of Maryland, I spent three and a half years as a DJ and music critic on WMUC, hosting a weekly hour-long show on everything from cultural influences on hip hop to one-hit wonders.

These days I bring that same curiosity to my work: digging into a big, complicated topic and finding the version of the story people actually want to hear.
